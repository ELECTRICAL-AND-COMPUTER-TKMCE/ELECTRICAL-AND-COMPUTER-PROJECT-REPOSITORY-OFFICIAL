## Project Description
Food waste and hunger are two major global challenges that coexist due to inefficiencies
in food distribution systems. Large quantities of edible food are discarded daily from
restaurants, hotels, and events, while many individuals and communities continue to face
food insecurity. This project aims to address this gap by developing a technology-driven
solution that enables efficient redistribution of surplus food.
The proposed system, FoodBridge, is a web-based platform designed to connect food
donors, Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs), and volunteers in real time. The sys-
tem follows a role-based architecture where each user category has specific functionalities.
Donors can post surplus food along with details such as quantity, expiry time, and loca-
tion. NGOs can discover and claim these donations based on their requirements, ensuring
controlled and organized distribution. Once a donation is claimed, a mission is automat-
ically generated, which becomes visible to volunteers who can accept and complete the
delivery process.
The system integrates GPS-based location services and provides structured work-
flows for both donation-based and request-based operations. It ensures transparency by
maintaining status updates throughout the lifecycle of a donation, from posting to final
delivery. The platform is implemented using modern web technologies, including HTML,
CSS, JavaScript for the frontend, PHP for backend processing, and MySQL for database
management.
The results demonstrate that the system successfully improves coordination, reduces
delays, and minimizes food wastage compared to traditional manual methods. The plat-
form provides a scalable and reliable solution that can be extended to larger regions and
integrated with additional features such as mobile applications, push notifications, and
advanced analytics.
The FoodBridge system aligns with global sustainability goals, including Zero Hunger
(SDG 2), Responsible Consumption and Production (SDG 12), and Climate Action (SDG
13). By leveraging technology to connect communities, the project contributes to reducing
food waste and improving access to food for those in need.
